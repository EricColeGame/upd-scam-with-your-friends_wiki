If the button is unresponsive due to a corrupted settings file, delete the control config profile located in your local game directory and restart the client to generate a fresh file.

### Can I bind secondary keys to a single action?
Yes, the updated control menu supports both primary and secondary inputs for almost every action. This allows you to have standard keyboard keys (like `E` for interact) paired alongside mouse thumb buttons (`Mouse 4`) so you can execute actions with whichever hand is most convenient in the moment.

### Why is my interaction key lagging during crowded multiplayer lobbies?
Interaction lag is typically caused by high server tick latency or key buffering conflicts. Ensure that interaction queuing is enabled in the gameplay settings, and verify that your primary use key is not shared with any background voice communication software or streaming overlays.

### What is the best key to assign for push-to-talk communication?
Most competitive players recommend using a dedicated mouse side button (`Mouse 4` or `Mouse 5`) or the `Left Alt` key. These positions allow you to speak instantly without lifting your primary fingers off the movement keys (`W`, `A`, `S`, `D`), preventing sudden stops while bluffing your opponents.
