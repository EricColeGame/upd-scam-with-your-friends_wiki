## Troubleshooting Control Glitches and Input Latency

Following significant updates, player experience indicates that keybind configs can reset, or input delays can crop up due to background frame-rate limits or overlay conflicts.

| Issue | Probable Cause | Recommended Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Input Lag / Floatiness** | V-Sync enabled or borderless window mode | Switch to Exclusive Fullscreen; disable V-Sync in-game |
| **Keybinds Resetting** | Config file set to read-only or cloud sync collision | Rebind, save, exit to desktop, and restart the game once |
| **Gamepad Unresponsive** | Steam Input conflicting with native drivers | Enable/disable Steam Input in game properties per controller |
| **Voice Key Stuck Open** | PTT toggle mode active instead of push-to-hold | Check Audio/Controls and toggle "Hold to Talk" mode |
| **Interact Double-Registering** | Mouse switch debounce latency or key repeat delay | Increase in-game input debounce window in settings |

If your custom bindings fail to register after applying a new patch, verify your local files and reset the control profile to defaults once before re-applying your preferred layout. This refreshes the underlying configuration schema.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I play UPD Scam With Your Friends with a controller on PC?
Yes, the game supports native gamepad input for standard Xbox and PlayStation controllers, as well as full remapping through Steam Input. You can customize stick sensitivity, trigger response curves, and button layouts directly from the in-game control options.

### What is the most reliable keybind layout for UPD Scam With Your Friends controls?
Most seasoned players recommend remapping interact to `F`, your stash to `Q`, and push-to-talk to a secondary mouse side button. This keeps your index and middle fingers dedicated strictly to movement (`W`, `A`, `S`, `D`), ensuring you never stop moving during heated negotiations.

### Why does my character keep dropping items accidentally?
The default drop key (`G`) is located immediately next to the primary interact key (`F` or `E` depending on default setups). Remap the drop or discard action to an out-of-the-way key like `X` or `Backspace` to completely eliminate accidental item drops during trades.

### How do I fix input lag during high-player multiplayer sessions?
Input latency in busy lobbies usually stems from uncapped frame rates or windowed display modes. Switch to Exclusive Fullscreen in video settings, cap your frame rate to match your monitor's refresh rate, and verify that peripheral polling rates are set to 500Hz or 1000Hz in your mouse software.
