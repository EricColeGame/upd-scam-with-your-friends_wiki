### 1. The Bait-and-Switch Liquidation
Place a premier-tier item into the trade screen alongside three low-tier filler items. Keep conversation lively by talking about a completely different player's current lead. During the final three seconds of the trade countdown, deselect the premier item and lock in. Panicked players frequently hit "Accept" without verifying the modified item grid.

### 2. The Artificial Shortage Gambit
Form an unspoken alliance with a second player in the lobby to corner a specific commodity class. Refuse to trade that commodity publicly, declaring that the endgame scoring multiplier heavily favors it. When other players scramble to acquire a piece of that inventory, sell your reserves at three times their standard valuation.

### 3. The Trojan IOU
Use the update's contract system to pledge assets you have no intention of holding by round five. Deliver immediate low-tier goods in exchange for their high-tier items, promising future returns. Community reports highlight that players who rely heavily on oral contracts often forget exact repayment conditions by late-game rounds.
